Help

Stuck at the side of the road? We can help.

Contact us

Email levi.bailey@hotmail.com and we'll reply within 2 working days. It helps to tell us your device (for example "iPhone 15" or "Samsung Galaxy A54"), the game version from Settings, and, for purchases, the store order number or the purchase ID in Settings > Grown-ups > Help.

Restore purchases

How do I get No Ads back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy it, open Chicken Cop and go to Settings > Grown-ups > Restore Purchases. Ads switch off straight away. You never pay twice.

Why didn't my coins come back?

Coins are used up when they're added, so the App Store and Google Play can't restore them, and Chicken Cop has no accounts to back them up. Coins, outfits and progress live on the device and are lost if the app is deleted. If coins you paid for went missing because of a problem with the game, email us with the order number and we'll put it right.

I paid but didn't get my coins.

Check your internet connection, then close and reopen the game. Unfinished purchases are completed the next time the shop opens. If they still haven't arrived after a few minutes, email us with the order number from the receipt email Apple or Google sent you.

Refunds

Apple and Google take the payment, so they handle refunds:

  • iPhone and iPad: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find the purchase and choose "Request a refund".
  • Android: go to your Google Play order history, find the purchase and choose "Request a refund" or "Report a problem". Purchases made in the last 48 hours can often be refunded straight away.

If a child bought something by mistake, tell the store that, and see how to stop it happening again.

Playing the game

How do I play?

Tap a glowing square to hop to it. Every time you hop, the cop chickens and the traffic move too. Reach the coop at the top without being caught or bumped by a car. Trick cops into the traffic to clear the way!

How do I get three stars?

Finish the level in fewer moves. The star bar at the top shows how many moves you can use for each star.

How do I unlock Night Mode?

Finish day level 10. Then tap the sun and moon switch on the home screen to play every level again in the dark.

How often do ads show?

A short ad (about 5 seconds) shows every 5 attempts at a level, never in the middle of a move. A grown-up can remove these for good with "No Ads" in Settings > Grown-ups. Reward videos (for coins or an extra life) only play if the player chooses to watch one. They stay available after No Ads.

Does Chicken Cop work offline?

Yes, every level can be played without internet. You need a connection to buy or restore purchases. No ads show when you're offline.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not yet. Progress is saved on the device. Phone-to-phone transfers (like an iPhone or Android backup restore) may bring it with them, but we can't promise it.
