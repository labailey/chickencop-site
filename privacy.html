1. Who we are

Chicken Cop is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Levi Bailey, trading as labailey Games, in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the information described here (the "data controller" under UK and EU data protection law). This policy covers the Chicken Cop app and this website.

2. What we collect, and what we don't

We don't collect

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, photos, voice, contacts, precise or approximate location from your device's GPS, or anything you type. The game has no sign-in, no accounts, no chat and no way to share content with other players.

Saved on your device only

Your progress (levels, stars, Night Mode), coins, the outfits you own and wear, sound settings, which tips you've already seen, how many attempts since the last ad, when the last hourly reward was collected, and the dates used to run coin pack offers. This stays on your device. We can't see it, and it's deleted when you delete the app.

Sent to our service providers

  • Purchases (RevenueCat). When you open the shop, buy something or restore purchases, the app sends RevenueCat a random ID made up by the app (not linked to your name or any account), the store's receipt or transaction details, the product, price and currency, your device type, operating system, app version and language, and your IP address (which is used to work out your country and currency). This lets us confirm purchases, unlock "No Ads", and restore it on a new device.
  • Ads (Google AdMob). When an ad is loaded, Google receives your IP address, device type, operating system, app version and how the ad was viewed or tapped. See section 5.
  • The stores (Apple, Google). Apple and Google handle downloads, payments, refunds and "Ask to Buy" under their own privacy policies.

We don't use analytics or tracking services, and we don't build profiles about players.

3. How it's used

  • To run the game and keep your progress on your device.
  • To process, check and restore purchases, and to deal with refunds and fraud.
  • To show a short, family-safe ad now and then, and reward videos when you choose to watch one, which keeps the game free. Ads are limited by how often they can show, and by what kinds of ads are allowed.
  • To answer you if you email us.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping purchase records.

If you're in the UK or EEA, our legal bases are: performing our agreement with you (purchases and running the game), our legitimate interests in funding a free game with non-personalised ads and in preventing fraud (balanced with children's interests, which is why ads are never personalised), and legal obligation (records we must keep).

5. Ads

Chicken Cop shows a short ad (about 5 seconds) every few attempts at a level, never in the middle of a move. Players can also choose to watch a reward video: one for 100 coins (at most once an hour), or one for a "Second life" after losing a level. Reward videos never start by themselves. All ads come from Google AdMob. In the app we:

  • mark every ad request as child-directed (under the US COPPA rule) and as for users under the age of consent (for the UK and EEA), so Google turns off personalised ads, remarketing and ad profiles;
  • limit ads to content rated suitable for general audiences ("G");
  • don't ask for permission to track on iPhone, and don't read the iPhone advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • on Android, remove the permission that lets the app read the advertising ID.

Google may still use limited information (such as IP address and device type) to deliver an ad, stop the same ad showing too often, measure that ads were shown, and prevent fraud. Grown-ups can switch off the ads between attempts with the one-time "No Ads" purchase. Reward videos stay available after that, but only play when chosen.

6. Purchases

Coin packs and "No Ads" are paid through the App Store or Google Play. A grown-ups check comes before any screen with prices. On iPhone, Family Sharing's "Ask to Buy", and on Android, Google Family Link, can also require a parent's approval. We receive confirmation of the purchase from the store through RevenueCat, never your payment details.

7. Children's privacy

Chicken Cop is made for players of all ages, including young children, and is designed to follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code (the "Children's Code") and the GDPR's rules for children:

  • We don't collect personal information such as names, emails, photos or location from children.
  • The only identifiers used are the random purchase ID and the device information the ad and purchase services need to work, prevent fraud and limit ad frequency (what COPPA calls "support for internal operations"). They aren't used to build profiles, track children across apps or show personalised ads.
  • Privacy settings are high by default, and there's nothing to turn on or off that would share more.
  • Links out of the app, purchases and this policy's link in the app sit behind a grown-ups check.

Parents and guardians can ask us what we hold about their child's device and have it deleted. See section 9.

8. How long it's kept

  • On your device: until you delete the app (or clear its data).
  • Purchase records at RevenueCat: for as long as we need them to provide and restore purchases and to meet tax and accounting rules, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner (the "No Ads" purchase can still be restored from the store afterwards).
  • Ad data at Google: as set out in Google's policies. Google says it doesn't use it to personalise ads for child-directed requests.
  • Emails to us: for as long as needed to help you, and no more than two years.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Switch off the ads between attempts: buy "No Ads" in Settings > Grown-ups.
  • Stop all data sharing: delete the app. Nothing is sent once it's gone.
  • Reset the purchase ID: deleting and reinstalling the app creates a new random ID.

Depending on where you live (for example in the UK, EEA or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your information, to object to or restrict how it's used, and to complain to a data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office). Because we don't know who you are, please email us the purchase ID shown in Settings > Grown-ups > Help (or the store order number) so we can find the right records. We'll reply within 30 days.

10. Where it's processed

RevenueCat and Google process information in the United States and other countries. Where information leaves the UK or EEA, they use the safeguards the law requires, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or the EU–US and UK–US data privacy frameworks.

11. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and we keep as little as possible. Only we can access our RevenueCat and AdMob accounts, protected by strong passwords and two-step sign-in.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If we ever want to collect something new about children, we'll update this policy first and, where the law requires, ask a parent's permission.
