About the ads

Ads help keep Chicken Cop free. We've set them up to be as gentle as we can:

  • A short ad every 5 attempts, only between attempts and never during the tutorial.
  • Every ad is marked as being for children, which tells Google to show only non-personalised ads, with no tracking across other apps and no ad profile built about your child.
  • Ad content is limited to the "general audiences" rating.
  • One "No Ads" purchase removes them on all your devices that use the same Apple ID or Google account.

If you ever see an ad you think is unsuitable, please email us the details and, if you can, a screenshot.

About purchases

  • Coins can be earned just by playing. Coin packs are an optional shortcut and only buy cosmetic items. They never make levels easier.
  • Every screen with real prices, and every purchase, is behind a grown-ups check (tap three numbers written as words, in order). Children see a "For grown-ups" note, never the price or a countdown.
  • Coins and outfits are saved on the device and can't be restored if the app is deleted. No Ads can always be restored.

Turning on purchase approval

  • iPhone and iPad: set up Family Sharing and turn on Ask to Buy, or block in-app purchases in Settings > Screen Time > Content & Privacy Restrictions > iTunes & App Store Purchases.
  • Android: use Google Family Link, or in the Play Store go to Settings > Authentication > Require authentication for purchases and choose "For all purchases".

About privacy

We don't collect your child's name, email, photo, voice or location, and there's no account. Progress stays on the device. The only information that leaves the device is what the ad and purchase services need to work and to stop fraud. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Links and settings

Links that leave the game (this website, the privacy policy, store pages) are inside Settings > Grown-ups, behind the same grown-ups check. Sound and music can be switched off in Settings.
