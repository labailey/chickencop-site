Hop across the road one square at a time, dodge the traffic and trick the cop chickens into it. Then waddle home to the coop!

Why did the chicken cross the road?

To get away from the cops, of course. Every level is a little puzzle with a big chase.

🐔

One hop at a time

Tap a glowing square to hop. Every time you move, the cop chickens move too, so think before you jump.

🚨

Out-fox the fuzz

Cops chase you when you get close. Lead them into the traffic and watch them get bonked off the board.

⭐

Three stars to win

Finish in fewer moves to earn more stars. Power-up bubbles help when the road gets busy.

🌍

Five worlds, 1,000 levels

Hometown, City, Motorway, Military Base and Space. 100 levels in each world, new tricks in every one, and every level again at night.

🌙

Night Mode

Earn Night Mode and play every level again in the dark, with a torch, street lights and sneaky shadows.

🎩

Dress-up wardrobe

Hats, colours, trails and sparkly FX skins, bought with coins you earn just by playing.

Take a peek

Bright days, spooky nights and a whole wardrobe of silly hats.

👪

A note for grown-ups

  • No sign-up, no chat, no names, no email addresses and no location. Progress stays on the device.
  • Anything that costs real money sits behind a grown-ups check.
  • Ads are kept short, family-safe and never personalised. One small purchase switches off the ads between levels for good. Reward videos (for coins or an extra life) only play if your child chooses to watch one.
